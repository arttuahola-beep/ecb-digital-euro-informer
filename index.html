Wednesday 4 November 2026 Banks

Pilot banks carry their own digital euro costs and customer liability, so budget the build and write your pilot terms now

The ECB's pilot FAQs set out the deal for the 36 payment service providers in the pilot. They bear their own costs and get no remuneration or reimbursement from the Eurosystem. They also may not charge consumers or merchants any fees for pilot payment services. This is a contract term in the PSP participation agreement, not law, and it applies only to the pilot.

Liability is split in two. Towards customers, the pilot PSP stays responsible under its own terms and conditions and the existing rules, such as PSD2, GDPR and the AML Regulation. Towards its national central bank, each side is liable only for wilful misconduct, including fraud, or gross negligence. The ECB says PSPs may adapt their existing customer terms for the pilot.

What to do: if you are in the pilot, price the build as a cost with no pilot income, and have legal draft the pilot terms and a fee-free customer notice now. If you are not in the pilot, note what the ECB says about reuse. It expects pilot investment not to be wasted, because the main interfaces to the central platform (DESP) are meant to stay stable. Whether that holds still depends on the final Regulation, which is in trilogue.
