On 30 October the ECB named the two network service providers that will connect banks and other payment providers to the digital euro platform. They are a group of Nexi Payments and Deutsche Telekom Business Solutions, and a group of Telefónica de España, Telefónica Global Solutions and Indra. The first contract has been in force since 28 September 2026 and covers the pilot. The second starts in May 2027.

Both providers are meant to be ready by 2029 for a possible issuance. Every payment provider that offers the digital euro will be able to connect through either one. That choice is now a real procurement decision for your bank, and it ties into your messaging setup, availability targets and exit terms.

This is a contract award, not law. The ECB will only decide on issuance after the EU regulation is adopted, and the next trilogue is set for 5 November. Still, if you are in the 36-provider pilot, or plan to be ready by 2029, start comparing both options now. Ask for connection specs, support hours and costs, and check how a switch between providers would work.
