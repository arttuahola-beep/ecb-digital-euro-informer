Who sets the digital euro holding cap is still open in the talks, so build your wallet limit as a setting you can change

The latest round of digital euro talks, on the night of 18–19 November 2026, ended without a deal. One open point is who decides the maximum amount a person can hold. The Council wants to keep the power to set a ceiling, based on an ECB recommendation, and Parliament does not yet agree. More talks are planned for 3 and 16 December. Nothing on the holding limit is final law yet.

Under the Council's position, the ECB would set the actual limit, but it must stay under a ceiling the Council fixes and reviews at least every two years. The Council's plan has a set order: the ECB announces a launch date, publishes a report, recommends a ceiling, and the Council decides within six months. If the Council does not decide, the ECB can still set the limit. In short, the exact number will come late, it may change over time, and no figure has been agreed.

For payment firms, the takeaway is simple: do not hard-code a limit. Build the cap as a central setting you can change quickly, with clear customer messages when a payment or top-up hits it. Test your wallet and funding flows at several possible limits now, so a late decision does not become a late launch for you.
