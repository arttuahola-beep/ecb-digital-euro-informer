The digital euro is not coming to replace cash. EU lawmakers are negotiating it in one Single Currency Package, together with a separate proposal on the legal tender of euro banknotes and coins. On 10 November, ECB Executive Board member Piero Cipollone told MEPs that the ECB supports strong rules on mandatory cash acceptance, access to cash across the euro area and resilient cash infrastructure. The sixth trilogue on 5 November focused on mandatory cash acceptance and its exceptions. These rules are still proposals, and the Irish Presidency is aiming for a political deal on 18 November.

For banks, the message is clear: do not plan to pay for your digital euro build by cutting cash services. Treat the two as one programme. Check your branch, ATM and cash-logistics plans against the access-to-cash rules as they take shape, and look at where digital euro and cash services can share channels, staff training and customer support.

Watch the 18 November trilogue closely. The final text should show how strict the cash rules will be and how they sit next to the digital euro duties. Cipollone said the issuance date can only be set once the Regulation is adopted, so a firm cash plan now will make your digital euro roadmap easier to defend later.
