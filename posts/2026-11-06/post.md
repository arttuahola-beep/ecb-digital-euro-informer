The Eurosystem’s new digital euro innovation-platform wave is still open, but only until 17:00 CET on Monday 9 November 2026. Payment firms, tech vendors and merchants can join Experimentation (January–June 2027) on e-receipts, multi-payer or multi-payee payments, conditional payments and app features, or Exploration on AI in payments and public-service uses. Joint applications that pair a PSP with a merchant are encouraged.

This is private-sector innovation on top of the scheme, not a change to the proposed Regulation. The Commission draft still bans programmable money; conditional payments are separate value-added services that fire when agreed conditions are met. The ECB will not pay participants, and selection results are due in January 2027.

If you want early insight into how the platform and rulebook can support paid add-ons, file one clear use case before Monday’s cutoff and name the partner roles you need.
