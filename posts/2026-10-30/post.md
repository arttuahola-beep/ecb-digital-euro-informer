The ECB is asking e-commerce and mobile commerce firms to join its digital euro preparations. Executive Board member Piero Cipollone said so on 24 October 2026. Today 36 payment service providers already work with the Eurosystem on a pilot. It is planned to start in the second half of 2027 and run for 12 months, with real payments made by ECB and national central bank staff.

This matters if you run online checkouts, wallets or in-app payments. The ECB says the digital euro should cover shop, person-to-person and e-commerce payments. Early players can help shape how online checkout works, and they learn the flows before rivals do. Late joiners will have to fit rules that others helped write.

Keep the legal status clear. The digital euro law is still a proposal, and trilogue talks between Parliament and Council are still going on. The ECB will only decide on issuance after the law is adopted. It aims for a possible first issuance in 2029 if the law passes by the end of 2026. So the step for now is small: name an owner, map your checkout and refund flows, and ask your national central bank or the ECB how to take part in the preparatory work.

— Lagarde
