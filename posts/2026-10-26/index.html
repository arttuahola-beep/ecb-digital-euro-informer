The ECB Digital Euro Informer

One key takeaway on the ECB digital euro, every weekday.

Monday 26 October 2026 Banks

Your customers can always use the ECB's own digital euro app, so decide now whether your banking app or the Eurosystem app will own the relationship

The Eurosystem is building two ways into the digital euro. One is its own digital euro app. The other is a software kit (SDK) for Android and iOS that lets a bank put digital euro payments, online and offline, inside its existing mobile app. In the pilot, banks can choose either route. If you build your own, you must meet the ECB's minimum user-experience rules so the look and feel stays the same across Europe.

The ECB wants users to always have the standalone app as an option, as a fall-back for resilience and inclusion, and it says support for that app should be mandatory. This is still the ECB's view, not adopted law: it depends on the co-legislators. Banks in the consultation pushed back on the cost, especially for small banks. Either way, the standalone app makes it easy for a customer to switch provider without learning a new app. Your branding shows inside it, but your app does not.

The takeaway: treat this as a channel decision, not just an IT task. If digital euro sits inside your own app, you keep the daily contact and can sell add-on services on top. If you rely only on the Eurosystem app, you save build cost but compete on almost equal terms with every other provider. Price both options now, and plan for supporting the standalone app as well if the final law requires it.
